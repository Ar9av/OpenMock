# Device model attribution

Openmock ships only models under **CC-BY 4.0**, which permits redistribution with
attribution. Sketchfab "Free Standard" models are deliberately excluded: that
license forbids redistributing the file as a standalone asset, which a public
repository does.

Add a row here whenever you add a GLB.

## Do not use

- **Sketchfab "Free Standard"** models, e.g. iPhone 16 by wimell and MacBook Pro 14 M5
  by apple-user. No redistribution, and both carry NoAI terms.
- **Apple Design Resources product bezels.** Their license forbids 3D renders and
  simulations of Apple products.

Device shapes are fan-made recreations. Openmock is not affiliated with or endorsed
by Apple, Google, or Samsung.
